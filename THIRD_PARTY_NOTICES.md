**Changes:** The included `examples/bike-rentals.csv` takes `dteday` and `cnt` from `day.csv`, renames them `date` and `value`, and adds `series_id = Bike rentals`. All other columns are omitted. No endorsement is implied. The data cover 2011–2012 and are not current.

## Synthetic demo data

The bundled static example is generated deterministically by `forecastlab/data.py`. It is fictional and does not represent a company's operations or realized business results.
